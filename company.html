About Multiflow

Communication infrastructure for India's next 10 million businesses

We started Multiflow because businesses were stitching together five different tools to do one job: reach their customers.

Mission

Make every channel as easy as sending one message

Whether it's a WhatsApp template, an SMS OTP or a voice reminder, businesses shouldn't need a different vendor, contract and dashboard for each one. Multiflow exists to collapse that complexity into a single, reliable layer — starting with WhatsApp and automation, live today.

Vision

A conversation layer for every business in India

We want Multiflow to be the default way small and large businesses alike talk to their customers — compliant, affordable, and genuinely easy to use.

Our story

Built by people tired of watching messages fail silently

Multiflow was started by a small team that spent years running marketing and support operations for growing businesses — and got tired of stitching together disconnected tools that never talked to each other. We're early: today, that means a WhatsApp Business API integration and a no-code automation builder, built properly on official infrastructure rather than rushed out. More channels are on the way, built the same way — one at a time, done right.
