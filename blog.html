Getting your WhatsApp green tick

The "green tick" is Meta's official business verification badge, and it's the single biggest thing that determines whether customers trust a WhatsApp Business number. Here's what actually matters:

  • Your business name must match exactly. The name on your WhatsApp profile needs to match your registered business name or a name you can prove you legally operate under — mismatches are the most common rejection reason.
  • Use a business phone number, not a personal one. The number needs to be reachable for verification and not already tied to a personal WhatsApp account.
  • Have your business documents ready. Meta may ask for a certificate of incorporation, GST registration, or other proof depending on your business type and country.
  • Category matters. Certain business categories (financial services, healthcare, alcohol) face extra scrutiny — expect additional review time.

Verification typically takes a few days once documents are submitted correctly. Multiflow handles the submission and follow-up on your behalf as part of onboarding.

5 WhatsApp automation flows worth setting up first

Automation is most valuable when it handles the repetitive, predictable parts of a conversation. These five flows cover the majority of what most businesses need on day one:

  • Welcome message. Instant reply the moment someone messages you for the first time, setting expectations for response time and next steps.
  • Order/booking confirmation. Automatic confirmation the moment a customer completes an order or books an appointment — no manual follow-up needed.
  • Reminder before a deadline. Appointment reminders, payment due dates, or renewal nudges sent automatically ahead of time.
  • Post-purchase check-in. A simple "how did it go" message a day or two after delivery/service — cheap to send, valuable for catching issues early.
  • Re-engagement for cold leads. A scheduled nudge to contacts who went quiet, without anyone on your team having to remember to follow up.

Each of these can be built in Multiflow's flow builder without writing any code — most take under 10 minutes to set up.

WhatsApp Business App vs. WhatsApp Business API — what's the real difference?

These are two genuinely different products, and it's worth knowing which one you actually need:

  • WhatsApp Business App is the free app you download on a phone. It's built for solo operators and small teams — one device, one person (or a small team using a shared phone), manual replies, basic quick-reply templates.
  • WhatsApp Business API (what Multiflow provides) is built for scale: multiple team members replying from a shared inbox, automated flows, template messages that need Meta approval, integration with your other tools, and usage-based pricing per conversation rather than a free app.

If you're a single person answering a handful of chats a day, the free app is genuinely fine. Once you have a team, need automation, or want to run broadcast campaigns, the API is the only path forward — that's the gap Multiflow exists to fill.
